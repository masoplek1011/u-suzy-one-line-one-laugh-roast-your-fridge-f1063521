A make.fun entry for **One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge**: a program that
writes a single roast line about a fridge to `entry.txt`.

The roast:

> My fridge hums like a nosy neighbour who watched me eat the last slice at 2 a.m. and is saving it for the group chat.

The fridge does not just keep food cold — it keeps receipts. Its hum is the
sound of a witness who saw the midnight snack raid and is waiting for the
right audience.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, fully deterministic) writes the one
line above to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and nothing else.
